Cloud and MQTT

Your phone and your devices exchange small messages through a cloud broker. Commands travel quickly, and status updates return as soon as a device changes state, so the app stays current.

IoT device integration

Lights, fans and water motors connect to the platform through compatible controllers. Each device reports its own state, which is what lets the app show accurate on, off and running indicators.

Intelligent automation

Rules and monitoring let the system act on conditions such as water level. It starts filling when needed and stops when the tank is full, then notifies you.

Frequently asked questions

What can I control with Aarohi AI?

You can control lights, fans and water motors, and monitor their status from one app. Intelligent Mode adds automation, such as automatic tank filling with tank-full auto-stop.

Where do I get the app?

The Android app is on Google Play. Use the Get it on Google Play button in the download section to open its page.

Do I need an internet connection?

Aarohi AI is cloud-connected, so an internet connection is used to reach your devices remotely and to receive real-time status and notifications.

How does the water motor automation work?

The system monitors the tank’s water level, can start the motor to fill the tank and stops it automatically when the tank is full. You can also switch the motor on or off yourself and see its real-time status.

Can I use it on more than one device?

Yes. Multi-device access lets you reach your smart home from more than one phone.

Is it secure?

Devices and the app communicate through secure, cloud-connected channels. We do not claim security certifications that have not been verified. For questions about data handling, contact us using the details below.
