Privacy Policy

Aarohi AI is a smart home automation platform that lets you control and monitor compatible devices such as lights, fans and water motors. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the Aarohi AI app and services. It should be read together with our Terms & Conditions.

The app uses MQTT for communication between the app, the cloud and your devices, and OAuth for sign-in and account linking.

1. Ways to use Aarohi AI

  • Guest Mode: you can use supported features without an account. Settings, preferences and other information may be stored locally on your device.
  • Registered account: you sign up with a valid Gmail/Google email address and the required account information. Cloud-connected services and advanced features may need an account.

2. Information that may be handled

  • Account information, such as your Google email address and the other information required to register.
  • App settings and preferences, stored on your device or, for registered accounts, on Cloudflare.
  • MQTT configuration or authorization details you provide so the app can reach your devices.
  • Device information and status, such as whether a light, fan or motor is on, and water level readings, so the app can show real-time status.
  • Notification information needed to deliver alerts through Firebase.

3. How information is used

Information may be processed to provide account authentication, device connectivity, cloud synchronization, notifications and smart home functionality, including automation such as water level monitoring and tank-full auto-stop.

4. MQTT connectivity

Aarohi AI uses MQTT for communication between the mobile app, cloud infrastructure and supported IoT devices. Control commands and status updates travel through this connection. Availability depends on your network and on MQTT services, which may be provided by third parties.

5. Sign-in and OAuth

Aarohi AI uses OAuth, an authorization standard, for sign-in and for linking your account with Google Home. You sign in on Google’s own screen and approve the access the app asks for. You can withdraw that access by unlinking your account.

6. Third-party services

Some features rely on external services, including MQTT infrastructure, cloud platforms such as Cloudflare, Firebase notifications and Google Home. These services have their own privacy practices and policies. Google Home integration is optional. If you link it, Google account authentication and permissions are handled under Google’s applicable policies, and you can unlink at any time. Unlinking does not delete your Aarohi AI account.

7. Storage and protection

Account data and related information are stored on Cloudflare. We aim to handle information carefully, but no system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Automatic deletion: if your account and its data go unused for 180 days, the data is deleted automatically. You can also request deletion earlier, as described below. Handling of deleted information follows this policy and applicable legal requirements.

8. Your choices and account deletion

  • You can stop using Aarohi AI at any time.
  • Uninstalling the app does not necessarily delete information already stored on external servers.
  • You can request deletion of your account and associated personal data through the account deletion process, subject to applicable legal retention requirements.
  • Data that has not been used for 180 days is deleted automatically.
  • Local app data can be cleared from your device settings.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services change. The latest version will be available on this page.
